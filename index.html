What is E.164 Format?

E.164 is the international phone number format required by Twilio: [+] [country code] [subscriber number]

✓ Valid: +14155552671 (USA)
✓ Valid: +525555551234 (Mexico)
✗ Invalid: (415) 555-2671
✗ Invalid: 14155552671 (missing +)
